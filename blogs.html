Sahitya Narayana

Cyber Security Analyst at Target

Getting the job was definitely a big moment for me. The journey wasn't easy—there were a lot of mock tests, practice sessions, and times I felt unsure—but I kept pushing through. The teachers really stood by me throughout, guiding me during each step and helping me stay focused. Cracking the group discussion (GD) was all about being confident, paying close attention to the rules and the topic, and making sure I presented my points clearly and logically. I also made sure I understood the subject well and expressed myself in a way that made sense to the panel. All of that, along with consistent preparation, helped me perform well in the interview and finally get placed.

Varun MS

Junior Software Engineer at Verizon

My selection process at Verizon was a great learning experience. The first round tested our aptitude and logical reasoning, which was quite straightforward with the right preparation. In the technical round, I was asked questions related to Java and Python, and the interviewer was very supportive, giving me time to explain my thought process. The final HR round was a friendly interaction focused on my interests, goals, and cultural fit. Overall, the process was smooth and encouraging, and it boosted my confidence. I genuinely felt that Verizon values both technical skills and a positive attitude.

Kusuma BM

Junior Software Engineer at Verizon

Getting placed in my very first interview was a proud and memorable moment for me. The journey wasn't easy—especially as a fresh graduate stepping into the professional world for the first time. It began with an aptitude test, which was quite challenging and really tested my problem-solving skills and speed. After clearing that, I moved on to the technical round where I was assessed on coding, SQL, and other core technical concepts. They didn't just evaluate technical knowledge—they were also keenly observing soft skills like communication, clarity of thought, and confidence. I made sure to stay calm, be clear with my answers, and show a willingness to learn. Overall, the placement process was a mix of nervousness, learning, and growth—but it ended with a great opportunity that marked the beginning of my career.
