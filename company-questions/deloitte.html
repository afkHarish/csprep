Q1. Find the HCF of 18, 108, and 264

Answer: C. 6
Explanation: To find the HCF of 18, 108, and 264, we can use the prime factorization method:
18 = 2 × 3²
108 = 2² × 3³
264 = 2³ × 3 × 11
The common factors are 2 and 3, and the lowest power of each is 2¹ × 3¹ = 6

Q2. Find the smallest 4 digit number, when divided by 7, 9 and 11 leaves a remainder of 3, 5 and 7 in each case

Answer: A. 1355
Explanation: Let the number be N.
N ≡ 3 (mod 7)
N ≡ 5 (mod 9)
N ≡ 7 (mod 11)
Using Chinese Remainder Theorem, we get N = 1355

Q3. If 43322K is exactly divisible by 72, what is K?

Answer: A. 4
Explanation: For a number to be divisible by 72, it must be divisible by both 8 and 9.
For divisibility by 8, last 3 digits must be divisible by 8.
For divisibility by 9, sum of digits must be divisible by 9.
Checking options, only K=4 satisfies both conditions.

Q4. Find the remainder if 2^39 is divided by 7

Answer: A. 1
Explanation: Using Euler's theorem:
φ(7) = 6
2^6 ≡ 1 (mod 7)
2^39 = 2^(6×6 + 3) = (2^6)^6 × 2^3
≡ 1^6 × 8 ≡ 8 ≡ 1 (mod 7)

Q5. Find the last digit of 587^1997

Answer: A. 7
Explanation: The last digit of 587 is 7.
The pattern of last digits for powers of 7 is: 7, 9, 3, 1, 7, ...
This pattern repeats every 4 powers.
1997 mod 4 = 1, so the last digit is 7.

Q6. A train running at 54 kmph takes 20 seconds to pass a platform. Next, it takes 12 seconds to pass a man walking at 6 kmph in the same direction in which the train is going. What is the length of the train and the platform?

Answer: A. 160m, 140m
Explanation: Let length of train be L and platform be P.
Speed of train = 54 kmph = 15 m/s
Speed of man = 6 kmph = 1.67 m/s
Relative speed = 15 - 1.67 = 13.33 m/s
L = 13.33 × 12 = 160m
L + P = 15 × 20 = 300m
Therefore, P = 140m

Q7. A boat takes 90 minutes less to travel 36 miles downstream than to travel the same distance upstream. If the speed of the boat in still water is 10 mph, the speed of the stream is:

Answer: A. 2 mph
Explanation: Let speed of stream be x mph.
Downstream speed = (10 + x) mph
Upstream speed = (10 - x) mph
36/(10-x) - 36/(10+x) = 1.5
Solving, x = 2 mph

Q8. A sum of money at simple interest amounts to Rs. 815 in 3 years and to Rs. 854 in 4 years. The sum is:

Answer: C. Rs. 698
Explanation: Let sum be P and rate be R%.
P + (P×R×3)/100 = 815
P + (P×R×4)/100 = 854
Subtracting, (P×R)/100 = 39
Therefore, P = 698

Q9. A man can row 6 kmph in still water. If the river is running at 2 kmph, it takes him 3 hours to row to a place and back. How far is the place?

Answer: A. 8 km
Explanation: Let distance be x km.
Upstream speed = 6-2 = 4 kmph
Downstream speed = 6+2 = 8 kmph
x/4 + x/8 = 3
Solving, x = 8 km

Q10. A sum of money becomes Rs. 13,380 after 3 years and Rs. 20,070 after 6 years on compound interest. The sum is:

Answer: B. Rs. 8,920
Explanation: Let sum be P and rate be R%.
P(1 + R/100)^3 = 13380
P(1 + R/100)^6 = 20070
Dividing, (1 + R/100)^3 = 1.5
Therefore, P = 13380/1.5 = 8920

Q11. A can do a piece of work in 4 hours; B and C together can do it in 3 hours, while A and C together can do it in 2 hours. How long will B alone take to do it?

Answer: C. 12 hours
Explanation: A's 1 hour work = 1/4
(B+C)'s 1 hour work = 1/3
(A+C)'s 1 hour work = 1/2
C's 1 hour work = 1/2 - 1/4 = 1/4
B's 1 hour work = 1/3 - 1/4 = 1/12
Therefore, B alone will take 12 hours

Q12. A train 110 metres long is running with a speed of 60 kmph. In what time will it pass a man who is running at 6 kmph in the direction opposite to that in which the train is going?

Answer: B. 6 seconds
Explanation: Speed of train = 60 kmph = 16.67 m/s
Speed of man = 6 kmph = 1.67 m/s
Relative speed = 16.67 + 1.67 = 18.34 m/s
Time = 110/18.34 = 6 seconds

Q13. A sum of money at compound interest amounts to thrice itself in 3 years. In how many years will it be 9 times itself?

Answer: A. 6 years
Explanation: Let sum be P and rate be R%.
P(1 + R/100)^3 = 3P
(1 + R/100)^3 = 3
For 9 times, (1 + R/100)^n = 9
(1 + R/100)^n = 3^2
Therefore, n = 6 years

Q14. A man can row 9 kmph in still water and finds that it takes him thrice as much time to row up than as to row down the same distance in the river. The speed of the current is:

Answer: B. 4.5 kmph
Explanation: Let speed of current be x kmph.
Downstream speed = (9 + x) kmph
Upstream speed = (9 - x) kmph
Let distance be d km.
d/(9-x) = 3 × d/(9+x)
Solving, x = 4.5 kmph
