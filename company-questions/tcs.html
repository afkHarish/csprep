Q1. If (x+10)% of 240 is 60% more than x% of 180, then 15% of (x+20) is what percent less than 25% of x?

Answer: B. 19 1/21
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
(x+10)% of 240 = 1.6 × (x% of 180)
(x+10)/100 × 240 = 1.6 × (x/100 × 180)
2.4(x+10) = 2.88x
2.4x + 24 = 2.88x
0.48x = 24
x = 50
15% of (x+20) = 15% of 70 = 10.5
25% of x = 25% of 50 = 12.5
Required percentage = (12.5 - 10.5)/12.5 × 100 = 16%

Q2. The value of 2 + 1/3+1/4+3/2 of 7/11 + (1 1/2 + 2 1/5 ) % 7 2/5 is:

Answer: B. 2 7/10
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
2 + 1/3 + 1/4 + 3/2 of 7/11 + (1.5 + 2.2) % 7.4
= 2 + 1/3 + 1/4 + 21/22 + 3.7 % 7.4
= 2 + 1/3 + 1/4 + 21/22 + 0.5
= 2 + 0.33 + 0.25 + 0.95 + 0.5
= 2.7 = 2 7/10

Q3. 6 1/3 + (5 5/6 ÷ 2 1/3 - 2 1/3 of 7 1/2) + 13 ½?

Answer: A. 6 1/3
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
6 1/3 + (5 5/6 ÷ 2 1/3 - 2 1/3 of 7 1/2) + 13 ½
= 19/3 + (35/6 ÷ 7/3 - 7/3 of 15/2) + 27/2
= 19/3 + (5/2 - 35/2) + 27/2
= 19/3 + (-15) + 27/2
= 19/3 = 6 1/3

Q5. The population of a town is 1,21,000 if it increases at the rate of 10% per annum, what is the difference between the population 3 years hence and that of 2 year ago?

Answer: B. 61055
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Population 3 years hence = 121000 × (1.1)^3 = 161051
Population 2 years ago = 121000 × (0.9)^2 = 98001
Difference = 161051 - 98001 = 61055

Q6. A retailer has fixed the Price of an article at 20% above the cost price. If he offers a discount of Rs 2 for a purchase of Rs 40, what is his gain or loss percentage?

Answer: D. 10% gain
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let CP = 100
MP = 120 (20% above CP)
Discount = Rs 2 on Rs 40
Discount % = (2/40) × 100 = 5%
SP = 120 × 0.95 = 114
Profit % = (114 - 100)/100 × 100 = 14%

Q7. A person gains Rs. 357 on selling a table at 13% gain and a chair at 6% gain. If he sells the table at 15% gain and the chair 8% loss, he gains Rs. 203. What is the actual cost of the chair?

Answer: C. 1400
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let cost of table = T and chair = C
0.13T + 0.06C = 357 ...(1)
0.15T - 0.08C = 203 ...(2)
Solving equations (1) and (2):
T = 2100, C = 1400

Q8. What sum of money at compound interest will amount to Rs. 6930 in 3 years, if the rate of interest is 5% for the first year, 10% for the second year and 20% for the third year?

Answer: C. 5000
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let P be the principal amount
Amount after 3 years = P(1 + 0.05)(1 + 0.10)(1 + 0.20) = 6930
P × 1.05 × 1.10 × 1.20 = 6930
P = 6930/(1.05 × 1.10 × 1.20) = 5000

Q9. If two numbers are, respectively, 25% and 40% less than the third number, what is the ratio of these two numbers?

Answer: D. 5:4
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let the third number be 100
First number = 100 - 25% of 100 = 75
Second number = 100 - 40% of 100 = 60
Ratio = 75:60 = 5:4

Q10. What is the sum (in Rs) which when divided among A, B, C, D in the proportion 2:3:5:8 provides Rs 8420 less to D the what it provides to him when the proportion is 1/2:1/3:1/5:1/8?

Answer: B. 25020
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
First proportion: 2:3:5:8
Second proportion: 1/2:1/3:1/5:1/8 = 60:40:24:15
Let total amount be x
(8x/18) - (15x/139) = 8420
Solving: x = 25020

Q11. A sum of money invested at compound interest amounts to Rs. 800 in 3 years and to Rs. 840 in 4 years. What is the rate of interest per annum?

Answer: C. 5%
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Interest for 1 year = 840 - 800 = 40
Rate = (40/800) × 100 = 5%

Q12. A train running at the speed of 60 km/hr crosses a pole in 9 seconds. What is the length of the train?

Answer: B. 150 meters
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Speed = 60 km/hr = (60 × 1000)/3600 m/s = 50/3 m/s
Time = 9 seconds
Length = Speed × Time = (50/3) × 9 = 150 meters

Q13. A boat takes 90 minutes less to travel 36 miles downstream than to travel the same distance upstream. If the speed of the boat in still water is 10 mph, the speed of the stream is:

Answer: A. 2 mph
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let speed of stream be x mph
Downstream speed = (10 + x) mph
Upstream speed = (10 - x) mph
36/(10-x) - 36/(10+x) = 90/60
Solving: x = 2 mph

Q14. A man can row 7.5 kmph in still water and he finds that it takes him twice as long to row up as to row down the river. Find the rate of stream.

Answer: A. 2.5 kmph
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let rate of stream be x kmph
Downstream speed = (7.5 + x) kmph
Upstream speed = (7.5 - x) kmph
Time upstream = 2 × Time downstream
d/(7.5-x) = 2 × d/(7.5+x)
Solving: x = 2.5 kmph

Q15. A boat takes 19 hours for travelling downstream from point A to point B and coming back to a point C midway between A and B. If the velocity of the stream is 4 kmph and the speed of the boat in still water is 14 kmph, what is the distance between A and B?

Answer: B. 180 km
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Downstream speed = 14 + 4 = 18 kmph
Upstream speed = 14 - 4 = 10 kmph
Let distance AB be x km
Time downstream = x/18
Time upstream = x/20
Total time = x/18 + x/20 = 19
Solving: x = 180 km

Q26. In a mixture of milk and water, the proportion of water by weight was 30%. If in the 40 litres of mixture, 10 more litres of water is added, what will be the percentage of water in the new mixture?

Answer: C. 44%
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Initial water = 30% of 40 = 12 litres
Initial milk = 28 litres
After adding 10 litres of water:
New water = 22 litres
Total mixture = 50 litres
New percentage = (22/50) × 100 = 44%

Q27. A shopkeeper bought 150 calculators at Rs. 250 each. He spent Rs. 2500 on transportation and marked up the selling price by 25% of the cost price. If he offers a discount of 10% on the marked price, what is his profit percentage?

Answer: B. 12.5%
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Cost price = (150 × 250) + 2500 = 40,000
Marked price = 40,000 + (25% of 40,000) = 50,000
Selling price after 10% discount = 45,000
Profit = 5,000
Profit percentage = (5,000/40,000) × 100 = 12.5%

Q28. If 6 men working 8 hours a day can complete a piece of work in 12 days, in how many days will 8 men working 6 hours a day complete the same work?

Answer: B. 12 days
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Using the formula: (M1 × D1 × H1) = (M2 × D2 × H2)
6 × 12 × 8 = 8 × D × 6
576 = 48D
D = 12 days

Q29. A train 150 meters long passes a pole in 15 seconds. If it passes a platform 200 meters long in 35 seconds, what is the speed of the train in km/hr?

Answer: A. 36 km/hr
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
For pole: Speed = 150/15 = 10 m/s
For platform: Speed = (150 + 200)/35 = 10 m/s
Converting to km/hr:
10 m/s = 10 × (18/5) = 36 km/hr

Q30. The average age of 8 men is increased by 2 years when two men aged 35 and 45 are replaced by two new men. The average age of the new men is:

Answer: C. 50 years
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let initial average age be x
8x = Sum of ages of all 8 men
After replacement:
8(x + 2) = (8x - 80) + y (where y is sum of ages of new men)
8x + 16 = 8x - 80 + y
y = 96
Average age of new men = 96/2 = 50 years
