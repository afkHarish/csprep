Q1. A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?

Answer: B. 12.5%
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let P be the principal amount
Amount after 8 years = 2P
Simple Interest = 2P - P = P
Rate = (SI × 100)/(P × T)
Rate = (P × 100)/(P × 8) = 12.5%

Q2. A man can row 24 km upstream and 36 km downstream in 6 hours. He can also row 36 km upstream and 48 km downstream in 8 hours. Find the speed of the man in still water and the speed of the stream.

Answer: B. 9 km/hr, 3 km/hr
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let speed in still water be x km/hr and stream speed be y km/hr
Upstream speed = (x - y) km/hr
Downstream speed = (x + y) km/hr
24/(x-y) + 36/(x+y) = 6
36/(x-y) + 48/(x+y) = 8
Solving: x = 9 km/hr, y = 3 km/hr

Q3. A train 200 meters long passes a platform 300 meters long in 25 seconds. If it passes a pole in 10 seconds, what is the speed of the train in km/hr?

Answer: A. 72 km/hr
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
For pole: Speed = 200/10 = 20 m/s
For platform: Speed = (200 + 300)/25 = 20 m/s
Converting to km/hr:
20 m/s = 20 × (18/5) = 72 km/hr

Q4. A contractor undertakes to do a piece of work in 40 days. He engages 100 men at the beginning and 100 more after 35 days and completes the work in stipulated time. If he had not engaged the additional men, how many days behind schedule would it be finished?

Answer: A. 5 days
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Work done by 100 men in 35 days = 35 × 100 = 3500 man-days
Work done by 200 men in 5 days = 5 × 200 = 1000 man-days
Total work = 4500 man-days
If only 100 men work: 4500/100 = 45 days
Days behind schedule = 45 - 40 = 5 days

Q5. A shopkeeper marks his goods 30% above the cost price and gives a discount of 15% on the marked price. What is his profit percentage?

Answer: A. 10.5%
Explanation: Let's solve step by step:
Let CP = 100
Marked price = 100 + 30% of 100 = 130
Discount = 15% of 130 = 19.5
Selling price = 130 - 19.5 = 110.5
Profit = 110.5 - 100 = 10.5
Profit percentage = 10.5%
